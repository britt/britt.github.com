A healthy liquid breakfast. 

### Ingredients

* 2 scoops of AG1
* 1 scoop of Organic Vanilla Whey Protein Powder
* 12 oz. of Coffee


Combine the dry ingredients in a bowl. Whisk them together. Then slowly add in the coffee, whisking as you go like you are making a roux. DROP IN AND DO A SICK BACKFLIP!!!
