Calvados and apricot brandy do the warming, Cardamaro brings the spice and a little maple sweetens the dose. Prescribed for snowy nights and long-winded relatives.

### Ingredients

* 2oz Calvados
* 1oz Apricot brandy
* 1/4oz Cardamaro
* 1/4oz Maple syrup
* 1/2 barspoon Juice from the cherries
* 3 dashes Aromatic bitters
* 3 Cherries

Stir everything gently with ice. Strain into a chilled martini glass and drop in the cherries.
